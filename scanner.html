A picture can start a better habit

Smart Waste Scanner 📸

Upload a picture, preview it, then confirm the closest item type to get disposal guidance. This prototype does not use automatic image recognition.

1. Add a photo

Use a clear image of one item. JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP images up to 8 MB are supported.

📷

Choose an image

Images stay in your browser for this demo.

2. Confirm the item

Select what is shown (used as fallback if AI is offline). AI vision will automatically identify your uploaded picture.

⚠️ Batteries, electronics, chemicals and sharp objects may require dedicated collection. Follow local authority guidance; never dismantle or mix hazardous materials.
