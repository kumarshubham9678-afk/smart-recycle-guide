A student sustainability project

Small actions. Greener future. 🌍

Smart Recycling Guide makes responsible waste disposal easier and more engaging through interactive digital guidance, educational games, and accessible recycling information.

Our goal

Make sorting waste feel simple, not confusing.

People often have questions about what belongs in recycling, what needs special handling and how to prepare items. This project brings a practical guide, a friendly demo assistant, a sorting game and a progress dashboard into one approachable experience.

The idea

From confusion to confident choices

🌎

The problem

Waste rules can be confusing, and mixed or contaminated materials can make recycling harder.

🧭

Our solution

Offer item-level tips, a guided scanner demo, an educational game and a searchable knowledge centre.

🌱

Learning outcome

Help people build better sorting habits while reminding them to follow local waste guidance.

What's next?

Future improvements

🧠

Real AI recognition

Connect a secure image recognition service and clearly communicate confidence and limitations.

📍

Local recycling rules

Provide verified, location-specific instructions and collection-point directories.

🎮

More learning activities

Add more quiz modes, educational content and accessible interactive challenges.

This prototype uses a local chatbot knowledge base and a user-confirmed scanner category. It does not claim to provide live AI recognition, verified local collection data or measured environmental impact.
